# Archived conversation — founder introduction and positioning origin

**Archive date:** 2026-09-28  
**Original conversation/event date:** Unknown from the available conversation transcript  
**Project:** Sentzunhat Corp / founder positioning  
**Status:** Historical precursor to the current Sentzunhat positioning

## Before

The spoken introduction began narrowly around helping businesses organize "cloud chaos," especially cloud files and tools such as Google Drive and OneDrive. The emphasis was on making digital information easier to find and manage.

## During

The framing was deliberately broadened beyond file organization:

- Custom software became the central service: solutions shaped around a company's needs rather than one-size-fits-all software.
- The core benefit became **control over the company's digital footprint**, including cloud tools, systems, workflows, files, and processes.
- Open-source work was added as evidence of the broader technical/product direction.
- **Human-AI Workflow Protocol (HAWP)** was introduced as a way to help teams collaborate/work more effectively with AI.
- **Zacatl** was explicitly clarified as a **"blazing-fast prototyping framework"** for developers to quickly create apps, services, or other software they need.
- Two audience levels emerged: a concise technical/business introduction and a simpler non-technical explanation.

The concise wording reached in the conversation was:

> I help companies tame their cloud chaos with custom solutions that give them control over their digital footprint. I’ve created the Human-AI Workflow Protocol to enhance team collaboration with AI, and I’ve built Zacatl—a blazing-fast prototyping framework that empowers developers to quickly build the apps or services they need.

For a less-technical audience, the framing became: help businesses get their digital files and processes in order, build tools that help teams work better with AI, and make technology less chaotic and more useful.

## After / current continuity

This conversation should be treated as an **early positioning milestone**, not the latest authoritative company description.

The durable progression is:

**cloud/file organization → custom software and digital control → HAWP + Zacatl/open-source tooling → broader Sentzunhat founder-builder/product-company positioning**

Later Sentzunhat positioning is broader: practical, privacy-conscious software; ownership/control; local-first and cloud workflows; AI-assisted tools; and simple systems with clear boundaries. That later framing supersedes this wording where they differ, while this checkpoint preserves how the message evolved.

## Strategic impact

The important shift was from describing a narrow cloud-file problem to describing a general philosophy: build custom software that reduces digital chaos and returns control to organizations, while open-source projects provide reusable foundations for human-AI work and rapid software creation.

This became an early bridge between Diego's consulting/custom-software explanation and Sentzunhat's later founder-led product-company identity.

## Continuation state

4. Use current repository/product definitions for HAWP and Zacatl rather than assuming this historical wording is still authoritative.
5. Keep a non-technical version available for general networking conversations.
