# 2026-09-29 — Sentzunhat El Salvador entity structure clarified

Repository: `sentzunhat/website`
Organization: `sentzunhat`
Branch: `main`

Privacy note: this repository is public. This checkpoint intentionally omits personal identifiers, private registry numbers, tax identifiers, bank details, and non-public ownership records. It records only public-safe continuation context for corporate planning.

## Before

Sentzunhat already had a Canadian corporate presence, and a future El Salvador entity was being considered as part of the broader Sentzunhat group strategy. Earlier planning left multiple possible models open:

- a Canadian parent company owning an El Salvador subsidiary,
- a Salvadoran branch or operating division of the Canadian company,
- a founder-controlled parallel El Salvador company,
- or a simpler personal/comerciante-style registration.

The El Salvador entity had not been formed. The practical setup path, naming convention, and relationship between the Canadian and El Salvador entities still needed clarification.

## During

The conversation clarified that the desired structure is not a Canadian-parent-to-El-Salvador-subsidiary model. The intended direction is a founder-controlled country-entity model: separate legal entities in Canada and El Salvador operating under the Sentzunhat brand family.

The term "division" should be treated as an operational or brand label, not as proof that one country entity owns the other. The El Salvador entity should therefore be incorporated or constituted locally if it needs its own tax identity, bank account, contracts, local invoicing, employees/contractors, or Hacienda/DTE access.

The preferred El Salvador entity type remains a local company, likely a Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (`S.A.S.`), subject to confirmation by Salvadoran counsel/accounting and name availability. A traditional `S.A. de C.V.` remains a possible alternative if a bank, investor, regulator, or advisor requires it.

Naming was also clarified. "Corp" can be used as a brand-style term only if accepted in the company name, but the legal name should still include the correct Salvadoran suffix, such as `S.A.S.` or `S.A. de C.V.`. A clean public/legal naming direction is something like `Sentzunhat El Salvador, S.A.S.`; a brand-facing name can remain `Sentzunhat Corp` or `Sentzunhat`.

## After / current state

The current planning state is:

- El Salvador should be a separate local legal entity if Sentzunhat needs local fiscal, banking, employment, contracting, or electronic-invoicing capability.
- The preferred first structure is a founder-controlled El Salvador company under the Sentzunhat brand family, rather than a Canadian-owned subsidiary or branch.
- The leading entity type to investigate is `S.A.S.`.
- `Sentzunhat El Salvador, S.A.S.` is the cleanest working name, while `Sentzunhat Corp, S.A.S.` remains a possible name only if accepted by Salvadoran naming rules and advisors.
- The company is not yet formed.
- Final legal, tax, banking, and name-availability decisions remain unresolved and require local professional review.

## Decisions and constraints to preserve

- Treat Canada and El Salvador as separate country entities under the same brand family unless new legal/tax advice changes the plan.
- Do not assume the El Salvador company is owned by the Canadian entity.
- Do not treat a personal/comerciante registration as equivalent to a separate company entity.
- Prefer a Salvadoran company form, likely `S.A.S.`, for startup/software operations unless a professional advisor identifies a reason to use `S.A. de C.V.` instead.
- Use "division" as a brand/operational label only, not as the legal structure.
- Keep the El Salvador entity's books, tax registration, bank account, contracts, invoicing, and compliance separate from the Canadian entity.
- Use written intercompany/service agreements if the country entities pay each other or share work.
- Use Salvadoran legal/accounting support before filing, especially for non-local founder setup, tax registration, powers of attorney, banking, and Hacienda/DTE requirements.
